Procedure

Rhinoplasty

Reshaping the nose for balance and comfortable breathing — planned around your anatomy and your goals, not a template.

Book a consultation Ask a question
A consultation in Nicosia is required before any procedure. Rhinoplasty is surgery: it carries risks and needs recovery time. Results vary between patients.
Clinical photo — consultation / 3D simulation (Sanity)

What rhinoplasty can address

Rhinoplasty can change the shape, size or proportions of the nose and, where needed, is combined with functional surgery to improve breathing. Whether the aim is aesthetic, functional or both is decided together at consultation. // REVIEW: medical copy

Your pathway

  1. In-person consultation and nasal assessment in Nicosia.
  2. Discussion of realistic options, risks and recovery — sometimes with 3D simulation.
  3. Surgery, if appropriate, with written pre- and post-operative instructions.
  4. Follow-up — in person, or by video for patients travelling from abroad.

Recovery at a glance

Visible swelling and bruising typically settle over the first weeks; full refinement takes longer. Exact timelines are individual and explained at consultation. // REVIEW: medical copy

Frequently asked questions

Will I need to stay in Cyprus?

Length of stay depends on the procedure and is confirmed at consultation; follow-up can often continue by video once you are home. // REVIEW: medical copy

Is the procedure covered by insurance?

Functional nasal surgery may be eligible depending on your insurer and country — discussed case by case.

Which languages can I consult in?

Greek, English and German.
